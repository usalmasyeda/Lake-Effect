**Demo rules:** going over 30 seconds or 7 slides makes us **ineligible for Global Judging**. A deck must show the **team name, every member's name and the challenge**. A video needs English subtitles if anyone speaks another language.

**Links:** host videos and documents elsewhere (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub) with **public access, no login**, and **test every link in a private browser window** before submitting. The page itself accepts images only (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB).

**Optional:** request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification for our website, so judges' networks don't block it.

**Certificate:** each person's certificate uses the **Full Name** in their profile: everyone should check theirs.

## 3. AI rules

- AI tools **are allowed**, but we must "clearly indicate where and how AI tools were utilized".
- AI-generated **images and videos** need a **visible watermark saying they are AI-generated** (team image, graphics, slide visuals). ❗
- AI-generated **code and data** must be acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata (README + code). ❗
- AI-generated content may **not use or modify NASA branding** (logos, insignia, mission identifiers). ✅ none used
- Judges weigh "originality, intent, and execution in the context of AI-assisted work".

## 5. How we're judged (2026 Judging & Awards Guide)

Every level of judging scores each criterion **1 to 5**:

| Criterion | What a 5 looks like | How we score high |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Impact** | Significant problem, broad reach, could change things | Lake Erie blooms affect drinking water and beaches; works for any lake |
| **Creativity** | Novel, or a meaningful improvement on existing ideas | Ranks every variable by *real* change, not just a trend line |
| **Validity** | Scientifically sound, fully developed, **usable and user-friendly** | Significance testing, NOAA-sourced definitions, honest "not significant yet" results, a working website |
| **Relevance** | Answers the challenge fully; **NASA data is core**, not superficial | Covers all four challenge questions; NASA MODIS chlorophyll drives the main result |
| **Presentation** | Clear, engaging, well-structured story (demo **and** full project) | Salma's Windsor beach story, 7-slide deck, clear project page |

The guide stresses giving presentation **equal** attention to the other four.

**Timeline:** Local judges pick Global Nominees (announced **Dec 3, 2026**) → expert judges pick Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions (**Dec 17, 2026**) → NASA and partner executives pick Global Winners (**Jan 26, 2027**, with a virtual celebration in early 2027). Local Leads may also give **local awards**: ask the Windsor organisers.

**Awards that fit us best:** Local Impact · Best Use of Data · Best Use of Science · Best Storytelling.

**Eligibility boxes we'll tick at submission:**

"I confirm that the submitted project represents my team's original work and that all external resources including code, text and images (even if they are open source or freely available) used in the project are listed in the References field … my team has not used any copyrighted materials … that we don't have permission to use."

Everyone who submits on time gets a participation certificate.

## 6. More 2026 rules (Use of SI and NASA Data fields)

- **At least one NASA dataset is required for a Global Award.** NOAA temperature doesn't count: **Mehak's NASA MODIS chlorophyll is what qualifies us.** MERRA-2 would be a second NASA source.
- **SI should not be used to come up with the idea of how to solve the challenge.** Our idea comes from Salma's experience in Windsor. Describe AI help honestly in the SI field (draft in `docs/submission-drafts.md`).
- The SI answer is for data collection only; **judges do see** the AI labels, watermarks and descriptive text in the project.
- AI help is disclosed in the README, the website footer, and a note at the top of each AI-assisted code file. ✅
- References with licences are in the README. ✅
- Space Agency Partner data (e.g. the **Canadian Space Agency**) is "highly encouraged" but optional.
- Judges score mostly the **project page**; the demo mainly affects **Presentation**.
